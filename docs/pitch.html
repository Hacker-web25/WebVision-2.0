Pravesh · pre-flight for India's e-Visa

The official site tells you what went wrong after you've paid.

The problem

A reactive system for a one-shot process.

Today

  • Apply, pay, then find out the photo or scan was wrong
  • Rules differ by passport; the route (e-Visa, VoA, mission) is on you to work out
  • Dates you must hit are not laid out anywhere in one place

With Pravesh

  • Know your route before you start
  • Photo and scan fixed to the published spec in your browser
  • One timeline: apply-by, arrival card, landing

Look at the form itself

Sensitive questions sit deep in a long form, not up front.

On the official sample form, family nationality and previous-nationality fields appear on page 3, and the six yes/no security declarations only on page 5. By then you've already spent your time.

Source: official sample form, indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/images/SampleForm.pdf (eTourist category, pages 3 and 5). Business and Conference categories not checked.

Pravesh

Ask once, up front. Get a plan, not a surprise.

  1. Passport and trip details in, personalised route out
  2. Document checklist for that route
  3. Timeline with apply-by, arrival-card and landing dates
  4. Save and share the trip; opt-in email reminders
  5. Payment evidence PDF kept for the day something goes wrong

What Pravesh never does

It is not the Government of India. It takes no payments. It never submits your application.

It just makes sure that when you do apply, you only apply once.

Pravesh is an unofficial companion. Rules data carries its source and date checked.
