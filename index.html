From passport to stamp.

It starts with your passport.

The country on the cover decides the route: an e-Visa, a visa on arrival, or a regular visa from an Indian mission.

Your photo, fixed before you pay.

Cropped square, set on white and compressed under 300 KB, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Approval arrives by email.

Once the fee is paid and the files pass, the approval comes by email before you fly. Print it and carry it with your passport.

Stamped on arrival.

The visa is stamped into your passport at the immigration counter. File the e-Arrival Card within 72 hours before landing.

    What Pravesh never does.
    
          
    It is a companion to the official site, built to be trusted with a passport. These limits are part of the design.
    
          Open the official e-Visa site 
        
    
        
      
          
    • Take a payment.
      There is no fee and no express service. The visa fee is paid only on the official site, and the official site says it charges no emergency fee.
      • 
          
    • Upload your files.
      Photos and passport scans are read and fixed in your browser. There is no server to send them to and no account to create.
      • 
          
    • Submit for you.
      You apply yourself, on indianvisaonline.gov.in. Pravesh prepares the answers, files and dates you will need there.
      • 
          
    • State a rule without its source.
      Each rule links to where it was published and when it was last checked. This build uses a demonstration set of 19 countries.
